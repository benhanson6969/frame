Your privacy, by design.

Frame was built to keep as little as possible. There are no accounts, chats live only in memory, and when a chat ends it is gone for both people. This page explains exactly what is processed and why.

01 The short version

Frame has two parts: private 1-on-1 chats and Frame Music, a song finder. Neither one asks who you are. The table below lists everything Frame handles.

DataWhere it livesHow long
Your chosen nameYour browser, and the server while a chat is openUntil you change it or clear browser data
Anonymous keyYour browser (the server sees only a scrambled ID)Until you clear browser data
Messages, photos, voice notesServer memory onlyUntil the chat ends (24 hours at most)
Song clip you recordSent to AudD to identify itNot kept by Frame
Visit statisticsServer memoryLast 1,000 visits, cleared on restart

02 No accounts, no profiles

The first time you open Frame, your browser creates a random private key and stores it on your device. The server turns it into a one-way anonymous ID so it can tell your chats apart. It cannot be turned back into the key, and it is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

The name you pick when you start a chat is a nickname. You can change it at any time.

03 Private chats

Messages, photos and voice notes are kept only in the server’s working memory while the chat exists. They are never saved to a database, a file or a backup.

  • A chat is locked to exactly two people.
  • The invite link works once and expires after 10 minutes.
  • Photos sent as view once disappear after they are opened.
  • Ending a chat erases every message, photo and voice note for both people, immediately.
  • Chats also erase themselves after 10 minutes with nobody connected, after 24 hours, or if the server restarts.
Frame cannot stop the other person from taking a screenshot or a photo of their screen. Only share what you are comfortable sharing.

04 Voice and video calls

Calls connect directly between the two browsers using WebRTC and are encrypted by the browser. Frame’s server only passes the short setup messages that let the two browsers find each other. Public STUN servers run by Google help with that step and can see your IP address.

05 Call alerts

If you turn on call alerts, your browser gives Frame a push address so it can tell you when your friend calls, even when Frame is closed. Frame keeps that address only in memory, linked to your anonymous ID, and forgets it when the server restarts. The alert itself is delivered by your browser’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple) and only says who is calling and whether it is a voice or video call, never your messages.

You can turn alerts off at any time in your browser’s site settings.

06 Frame Music

When you tap to identify a song, a short audio clip (about 10 seconds) is recorded and sent to AudD, which tells us the song. Frame does not keep the clip. Microphone access is only used while the listening screen is open, and your browser always asks first.

Charts, song details, 30-second previews and similar-song suggestions come from Deezer. Previews play straight from Deezer’s servers. Lyrics, when shown, come from Musixmatch.

07 What stays on your device

Frame uses your browser’s local storage, not tracking cookies, to remember:

  • your anonymous key and chosen name,
  • your language and Frame Music settings,
  • your list of found songs and recent searches.

All of this stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing your finds in Settings or clearing your browser data for this site.

08 Visit statistics

To understand basic use, the server notes each visit: IP address, approximate city and country, browser, operating system and device type. The location is looked up through ip-api.com. This list holds only the last 1,000 visits, lives in memory, is visible only to the site owner, and is erased whenever the server restarts.

10 Your choices

Because Frame holds no account and almost nothing about you, most of your control is in your hands:

  • End any chat to erase it for both people.
  • Change your name at any time.
  • Clear your finds and searches in Frame Music settings.
  • Clear your browser data to remove your key and everything stored on your device.
  • Block the microphone in your browser if you do not want to use song recognition.

11 Children

Frame is not meant for children under 13. If you are under the age required in your country, use Frame only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change too. Important changes will be shown in the app.
