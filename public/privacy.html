> PRIVACY POLICY

Template: review and adapt with a legal professional before relying on it. Last updated: 2026-11-16.

What we store

No accounts: we never ask for your email, phone number or real name. Your browser keeps a random private key and the name you choose; the server only sees an anonymous ID derived from that key.

Chats: messages, photos and voice notes are kept only in the server's memory while the chat exists, and never written to a database or disk. They are erased when the chat ends.

Visitor statistics

When you open the site we record your IP address, approximate city/country, browser and device for basic statistics. This list is kept in memory (last 1,000 visits), visible only to the site owner, and cleared when the server restarts.

Frame Music

When you use Frame Music, the short audio clip you record or upload is sent to AudD (audd.io) to identify the song. We do not keep the clip.

Calls

Voice and video calls connect directly between the two browsers (WebRTC). Public STUN servers from Google help the browsers find each other.

Your choices

You can end any chat at any time to erase it. Clearing your browser data removes your key and name from your device.
