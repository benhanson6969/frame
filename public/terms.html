Frame has no sign-up or login. Your browser keeps a random private key that identifies your chats. If you clear your browser data, you lose access to your open chats.

4. Ephemeral chats

Chats are deleted when either person ends them, after 10 minutes with nobody connected, after 24 hours, or when the server restarts. Deleted chats cannot be recovered. The other person can still take screenshots or photos of their screen.
