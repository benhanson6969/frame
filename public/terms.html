Frame offers private chats between two people, joined with a one-time link or code, and Frame Music, a tool that identifies songs and shows charts, previews and similar songs. Frame is free and needs no account.

04 Chats that disappear

Chats are temporary by design. A chat is erased when either person ends it, after 10 minutes with nobody connected, after 24 hours, or when the server restarts. Erased chats cannot be recovered, by you or by us, so do not use Frame to keep anything you need later.

The other person can still take screenshots or photos of their screen. You are responsible for what you choose to send.
